## The Problem

Every day, 400M+ Indian families in Tier 2-3 suburbs lose 40-60 minutes juggling 3-4 apps and shops for basic needs.

- **Kiranas**: Trust exists, but 50% stockouts, no services, no tech
- **Quick Commerce**: Fast but faceless — 20-30% quality failures, zero relationships
- **Supermarkets**: Far, impersonal, staff rotates, nobody knows your name
- **Result**: Zero trusted advisors. Nobody remembers the customer.

> DMart knows your bill amount but not your name. Zepto knows your address but not your allergies. The kirana knows your face but can't stock what you need.

## The Insight

**This isn't a technology problem. It's a relationship problem.**

I grew up running a kirana store with my father. I've watched customers stay loyal for decades — not because of prices, but because someone remembered them, extended credit when times were hard, and asked about their family.

Then I travelled to Thailand and Taiwan. I saw 7-Eleven and FamilyMart — convenience stores on every corner, combining products + services + speed. But they had no soul. Nobody knew your name.

**Consto combines both**: The warmth of an Indian kirana + the efficiency of an Asian convenience store + AI that remembers every customer like family.

## The Solution

**Consto** is a 600-800 sq ft AI-powered neighbourhood store where every customer gets a personal agent who remembers their preferences, anticipates needs, and solves daily life.

### How It Works

- **Personal Agents**: Each customer is assigned a human agent (backed by AI) who knows their family, diet, festivals, sizes, and habits
- **Proactive Care**: Agent messages "Milk running low — reserved for you?" before the customer realises
- **One-Stop Services**: Groceries + AEPS cash withdrawal + bill payments + recharges + pharmacy basics — all in one visit
- **Community Hub**: Notice board, festival calendar, monthly events, chai counter, "Come rest without buying" policy

### What Makes It Indian

- Credit with dignity when pension is delayed
- Festival-specific bundles (Ugadi pachadi kit, Sankranti til pack)
- Telugu/regional language communication
- Pilgrim travel help researched by agents
- Hyperlocal customisation — colony, highway, and premium variants
