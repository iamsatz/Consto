# Consto — Research Plan

Status: draft · Owner: Sateesh · Feeds: 60-day memory pilot (`BACKLOG.md`)

**Rule:** research answers one question first — *does being remembered make people come back more?* Everything else (fresh food, formats, hybrid) is a second round.

---

## 1. Users

| Group | Who | What they need from Consto | Priority now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary shopper | Household decision-maker (often the mother/homemaker) | Daily top-ups, fresh basics, credit, trust | **High** |
| Working professional | 24–40, IT/office, time-poor | Fast, late hours, ready-to-eat, UPI | High |
| Elders | 60+, low digital comfort | Being known, home delivery, pension/AEPS | Medium |
| Students / bachelors | Hostels, PGs | Cheap meals, recharges, snacks | Medium |
| Store staff | Cashier, helper | Simple POS, "who is this customer" in 1 glance | **High** (they use the OS) |
| Store owner | You now; kirana owners later (B2B) | Margin, repeat customers, less stock waste | **High** for the software bet |
| Suppliers | Distributors, local farmers, dairies | Predictable orders, on-time payment | Low (later) |
| Service partners | Couriers, bill-pay, AEPS, tailors | Footfall and commission | Low (later) |
| Investors | Seed / angel | Proof of repeat + unit economics | Pitch thread |

## 2. Category

- **Primary:** Organised convenience retail (neighbourhood format).
- **Secondary:** Retail-tech / vertical SaaS for kiranas (the long-term bet).
- **Adjacent:** Phygital / hybrid retail, local services hub, CRM for small merchants.
- Pick **one** for each audience: investors → retail-tech; shoppers → "your neighbourhood store that knows you".

## 3. Design process (6 steps, ~12 weeks)

1. **Frame** (week 1) — write the 3 riskiest assumptions + a pass/fail number for each.
2. **Listen** (weeks 1–3) — 20 shopper interviews, 5 kirana owners, 5 store-staff shadows. Record, transcribe, tag.
3. **Watch** (weeks 2–3) — 3 × 2-hour observation slots at local kiranas and a supermarket (morning, evening, Sunday). Count visits, basket, time, what's asked for.
4. **Synthesise** (week 4) — affinity map → 3 personas → 1 journey map per format → "How might we" list.
5. **Test cheaply** (weeks 5–12) — the ₹0 memory pilot (Google Sheet + human-sent messages, control group).
6. **Decide** (week 12) — build / change / stop, against the numbers written in step 1.

## 4. Research questions

### A. Shoppers — habits
1. Walk me through the last three times you bought groceries. Where, what, why there?
2. What do you buy daily vs weekly vs monthly?
3. Where do you buy vegetables, milk and curd? What time? From whom?
4. When did you last use Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart? For what? Why not the local shop?
5. Which shop do you trust most? What did they do to earn it?

### B. Shoppers — memory (core hypothesis)
6. Has a shopkeeper ever remembered something about you? How did it feel?
7. Would you want a store to remember your usual items? What would feel creepy?
8. Would you reply to a message from a shop? What would make you ignore it?
9. What would make you switch your main shop?

### C. Shoppers — fresh and food
10. What do you refuse to buy packaged? Why?
11. Would you pre-order fresh items (veg, milk, idli batter) the night before for morning pickup?
12. What ready-to-eat food would you buy from a store near office/home? At what price?

### D. Services and hybrid
13. Which errands do you do outside the house in a week (bill pay, courier, cash withdrawal, recharge)?
14. Would you do them at your grocery store? Which ones would you not trust a store with?

### E. Kirana owners (B2B)
15. How do you remember regular customers today? What happens when you're not at the counter?
16. How do you track credit (khata)? How much is unpaid right now?
17. Which software have you tried? Why did you stop?
18. What would you pay per month for more repeat customers? Who decides the purchase?

### F. Store staff
19. What slows billing down? What do customers ask that you can't answer?

**Interview rules:** ask about past behaviour, not future opinions. Never pitch Consto during the interview. "Would you use…?" answers are weak; "last time you…" answers are strong.

## 5. Fresh food by location (hypotheses to test)

| Location | What "fresh" means there | Model | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential (Beeramguda) | Ingredients to cook: veg, milk, curd, batter, eggs | Pre-order + partner with local vendor/dairy; small daily display | Spoilage; street vendors deliver to the door |
| IT parks / offices | Ready-to-eat: meals, tiffin, fruit bowls, coffee | Tie-up with a cloud kitchen; lunch pre-order | Canteens, Swiggy |
| Highways | Hot + safe: chai, snacks, clean toilets, water | Packaged + a small hot counter | Low repeat, only one-time buyers |
| Premium / luxury areas | Imported, organic, gifting | Packaged premium; fresh only on order | Small market, high rent |

Note: offices need fresh **more**, not less — just ready-to-eat, not raw.

## 6. Hybrid store — what makes it different from a supermarket

| Supermarket | Consto hybrid |
|---|---|
| 5,000+ sqft, drive-to, weekly shop | 1,000–1,500 sqft, walk-to, daily top-up |
| Anonymous checkout | Knows your name, usuals, family |
| Goods only | Goods + services (AEPS, bill pay, courier pickup) |
| No credit | Digital khata for trusted regulars |
| Fixed stock | Pre-order fresh, pickup or delivery |
| Loyalty = points | Loyalty = being remembered |
