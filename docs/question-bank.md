# Consto — Question Bank

Every question from Thread 0 (`Reference/threads/consto-0-idea-discussion.md`) and `docs/research-plan.md`, in one place, grouped by who you ask.

**Priority**
- **P1** = ask now, before or during the 60-day pilot. It tests the core idea: *does being remembered make people come back more?*
- **P2** = ask in round 2, after the pilot result.
- **P3** = only if you pursue that format or location.

**New** = added here to cover the 4 locations and the supermarket comparison. Not in the earlier files.

## Part B: Field research (ask other people)

**Rules for every interview**
- Ask about past behaviour ("last time you…"), not future opinions ("would you…").
- Never pitch Consto during an interview.
- Record (with permission), transcribe, tag.
- Target: 20 shoppers, 5 kirana owners, 5 store staff.

### B1. Shoppers: habits

| # | Question | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| B1.1 | Walk me through the last three times you bought groceries. Where, what, why there? | P1 |
| B1.2 | What do you buy daily vs weekly vs monthly? | P1 |
| B1.3 | Where do you buy vegetables, milk and curd? What time? From whom? | P1 |
