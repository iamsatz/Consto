# Consto — Pitch Question Bank

Internal. Seed ask: ₹30–50L. 100 investor questions + 40 research questions.
Grade each answer: 🟢 strong · 🟡 weak · 🔴 red flag · ⬜ not answered yet.

⭐ = likely asked in the first 10 minutes. Prepare these first.

---

## Part A — Investor questions (100)

### 1. The big "why" (1–10)
1. ⭐ Why open a store instead of proving the software in 5 existing kiranas for ₹0?
2. ⭐ Are you a retailer or a software company? Pick one for this round.
3. Explain Consto in one sentence to a kirana owner in Gadwal.
4. What do you believe about Indian retail that most people think is wrong?
5. Why now? What changed in the last 2 years that makes this possible?
6. Why you? What unfair advantage do you have over a retail operator?
7. If Consto dies in 18 months, what's the most likely cause?
8. What's the one metric that tells you this is working?
9. What would make you quit and do something else?
10. Why Beeramguda and not Gachibowli, Kondapur or a Tier-2 town?

### 2. Customer and problem (11–22)
11. ⭐ Who exactly is your customer? Describe one real person.
12. What does that customer do today when they need milk at 10pm?
13. What pain are you solving that a kirana uncle doesn't already solve by knowing their name?
14. ⭐ How many customer interviews have you actually done? Show me the notes.
15. What surprised you most in those interviews?
16. Did anyone say "I'd pay more" or "I'd switch" for memory? How many?
17. Does "the store remembers me" feel creepy to some customers? What did they say?
18. Who is *not* your customer?
19. What share of the neighbourhood's grocery spend is already on Blinkit, Zepto or Instamart?
20. What do customers value more: price, speed, trust, or credit (khata)?
21. Will you give credit? If not, why would kirana loyalists switch?
22. How big is the catchment? How many households within 500m?

### 3. Validation and traction (23–32)
23. ⭐ Has the 60-day ₹0 pilot started? What's the result so far?
24. What number in the pilot kills the idea? Be exact.
25. Who are the "known contacts"? Isn't that a biased sample?
26. How will you separate the effect of memory from the effect of you being a friend?
27. Any letter of intent, pre-order or waitlist?
28. Any kirana owner who said "I'd pay ₹X/month for this software"?
29. What have you shipped so far? What's live?
30. What did you try that failed?
31. What would you need to see to stop the store plan and go software-only?
32. How long before you have repeat-visit data you'd bet money on?

### 4. Unit economics (33–47)
33. ⭐ Your model shows about −₹18K/month at 120 bills/day. Why should I believe you'll reach 137?
34. Where does 120 bills/day come from? A comparable store, or a guess?
35. What's your average bill value, and what's it based on?
36. What gross margin by category: staples, FMCG, fresh, services?
37. How much fresh produce do you expect to throw away?
38. Rent per sqft, and how many years is the lease locked in?
39. Staff: how many people, what salary, which shifts?
40. Electricity for chillers: what's the monthly bill?
41. ⭐ The 10% agent commission eats about 55% of gross margin. Why keep it?
42. How many months to operational break-even? To capex payback?
43. What's customer acquisition cost for a walk-in store? How do you measure it?
44. What's lifetime value of one household?
45. How does working capital (stock days, supplier credit) affect cash?
46. What happens to the model if UPI MDR or FMCG distributor margins change?
47. Is the "~40% revenue from services" the transaction value or the commission? Show both.

### 5. The ask and use of funds (48–56)
48. ⭐ ₹30L vs ₹50L: what exactly changes between the two?
49. Line by line: capex, stock, deposit, salaries, software, marketing.
50. How many months of runway does this buy?
51. What milestones will you hit before the money runs out?
52. What valuation are you asking for? How did you arrive at it?
53. Equity, SAFE, or convertible note? Why?
54. Who else is investing? Friends and family? Your own money?
55. How much of your own money is in?
56. What's the next round, how much, and what must be true to raise it?

### 6. Competition (57–68)
57. ⭐ Blinkit or Zepto deliver here in ~10 minutes (verify). Why walk to you?
58. SuperK and Apna Mart franchise small-format stores. How are you different?
59. 7-Eleven India (Reliance) and Twenty Four Seven exist. Why haven't they won?
60. DMart and Ratnadeep are nearby (verify). How do you compete on price?
61. What stops the local kirana from copying your memory idea with a notebook?
62. Khatabook, OkCredit, Vyapar, and many POS players serve kiranas. Why would kiranas buy yours?
63. Jumbotail, Udaan and ONDC are digitising kiranas. Where do you fit?
64. What's your moat in year 1? In year 3?
65. Is "memory" a feature or a company?
66. If Reliance copied you tomorrow, what would you do?
67. Who is the closest company to what you're building, anywhere in the world?
68. Your public site said "zero organised chains". Why did you believe that?

### 7. Product and Consto OS (69–78)
69. Eight products for a store that isn't open. Why not one?
70. What's the minimum software the pilot needs? Can it be a Google Sheet?
71. Buy or build the POS? Why?
72. What does AI do that a well-designed CRM can't?
73. What does it cost per store per month in Claude API, SMS and hosting?
74. How do you handle DPDP Act consent, phone encryption and deletion?
75. What happens when the internet or power goes out?
76. Who builds the software? You, freelancers, or AI tools?
77. How do you stop the store from being a product demo instead of a business?
78. Which one product would a kirana pay for first?

### 8. Team and execution (79–88)
79. ⭐ Who runs the store day to day?
80. Have you ever run retail, managed stock or handled cash?
81. Who's your co-founder? If none, why not?
82. Are you full-time on this? What's your personal runway?
83. Who are your advisers, and what have they actually done for you?
84. What's the one hire you need first?
85. How will you prevent staff theft and stock leakage?
86. Who negotiates with FMCG distributors?
87. What do you do on day 1 if footfall is half of your plan?
88. What's your biggest weakness as a founder?

### 9. Scale and exit (89–95)
89. Store 2: when, where, and what triggers it?
90. Company-owned, franchise, or software-only for scale?
91. What does a 10-store Consto look like financially?
92. If it's software: price per kirana, number of kiranas, ARR in 3 years?
93. Who would buy Consto one day?
94. What return do you think I'll make, and in how many years?
95. What's the Tier-2 story (Gadwal) vs the metro story?

### 10. Risk and trust (96–100)
96. Your GitHub repo is public and links an internal strategy doc. What else is exposed?
97. What legal licences do you need (FSSAI, trade, GST, shop act)? Status?
98. What if the landlord doesn't renew in year 3?
99. What claims on your site can you not back up today?
100. Why should I trust your numbers after the "zero chains" and "80K Japan" errors?

## Part B — Research questions (40)

Rule: every answer needs a link and a date. No source = don't use it.

### Local market: Beeramguda (R1–R10)
R1. Which quick-commerce apps deliver to Beeramguda pin codes? Delivery times?
R2. Nearest DMart, Ratnadeep, More, Vijetha, Reliance Smart? Distance?
R3. How many kiranas within 1 km of the planned site? (Walk and count.)
R4. Household count and income profile in the catchment (census, RERA project lists).
R5. Commercial rent per sqft on the main road?
R6. Footfall at peak hours at 2–3 nearby kiranas (count for 1 hour).
R7. What services do nearby kiranas already offer (recharge, AEPS, bill pay, courier)?
R8. Do nearby kiranas give credit? To what share of customers?
R9. New gated communities coming up nearby in the next 2 years?
R10. What do 20 residents say is missing from their neighbourhood?

### Competitors (R11–R22)
R11. SuperK: store count, store size, model, funding, founders.
R12. Apna Mart: store count, franchise economics, funding.
R13. 7-Eleven India (Reliance): store count, cities, status.
R14. Twenty Four Seven: store count, is it growing or shrinking?
R15. Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart: dark store count in Hyderabad.
R16. Khatabook, OkCredit, Vyapar: users, pricing, what they charge kiranas.
R17. Kirana POS players (e.g. GoFrugal, Snapbizz, others): pricing, reach, what happened to them.
R18. Jumbotail, Udaan: what software do they give kiranas?
R19. ONDC: how many kiranas are live as sellers?
R20. Any AI-for-kirana startups funded in 2024–26?
R21. Japan konbini: verified store count (source: JFA monthly data).
R22. Global examples of "store as software dogfood" (e.g. Amazon Go, others): what worked?

### Market size (R23–R30)
R23. Number of kiranas in India (cite report + year).
R24. India grocery retail market size and organised share.
R25. Convenience store segment size in India.
R26. Quick-commerce GMV in India and growth rate.
R27. Kirana-tech / retail SaaS market size in India.
R28. What share of kiranas use any digital POS today?
R29. Average kirana revenue and margin (cite survey).
R30. How many kiranas in Telangana / Hyderabad?

### Funding and comparables (R31–R36)
R31. Seed rounds for Indian retail/kirana-tech, 2023–26: amount, valuation, investors.
R32. Angel networks active in Hyderabad retail/consumer (e.g. T-Hub, others).
R33. Government grants: T-Hub, WE-Hub, Startup India Seed Fund eligibility.
R34. What do investors expect at pre-seed vs seed in India today?
R35. Revenue-based financing or bank loans (MUDRA) as an alternative to equity for the store?
R36. Typical dilution at ₹30–50L seed in India.

### Operations and regulation (R37–R40)
R37. Licences for a convenience store in Telangana: list, cost, time.
R38. AEPS / banking correspondent rules and real commission rates.
R39. DPDP Act 2023 rules: what applies to a store with a customer database?
R40. FMCG distributor margins and credit terms for a new small store.
