# Product Mix and Category Structure: 7-Eleven and FamilyMart (Japan, Thailand, Taiwan, USA)

Research date: 2026-10-06. Fiscal-year conventions: Seven-Eleven Japan (SEJ) FY2024 = year ended Feb 2025; FamilyMart FY2024 = year ended Feb 2025; CP All and PCSC use calendar years; 7-Eleven Inc (US) uses calendar years. All figures carry the year of the source. "Estimate" or "inference" flags mean the number was derived by me, not published.

## Q1. Seven-Eleven Japan: full category tree, share of sales, gross margin by category

### Takeaway
SEJ publishes a four-bucket split (processed food / fast food / daily food / non-food). In FY2024 food was 68% of merchandise sales but ~80% of gross profit, because food categories earn 35–41% gross margin while non-food (dominated by cigarettes) earns under 20%. Chain-wide merchandise gross margin was 32.0% in FY2024.

### Cited Findings

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY2019 | 1,297,660 | 1,533,143 | 661,356 | 1,518,112 | 5,010,271 |
| FY2022 | 1,311,714 | 1,523,448 | 645,528 | 1,683,539 | 5,164,229 |
| FY2023 | 1,426,539 | 1,565,976 | 670,366 | 1,700,049 | 5,362,930 |

**What sits inside each SEJ bucket** (category definitions as used in Seven & i Corporate Outline editions; the FY2024 page fetch returned the numbers but not the footnotes, so definitions are from earlier editions of the same document series)

- Fast food: rice items (onigiri, bento/boxed lunches, sushi), noodles, sandwiches, delicatessen/salads, counter fried items, oden, Seven Café — same source
- Daily food: bread/pastries, dairy, desserts/sweets, chilled items with daily delivery — same source
- Non-food: cigarettes, magazines/books, daily goods (toiletries, stationery, batteries), cosmetics, OTC items where licensed — same source
- Note: FamilyMart defines "fast food" much more narrowly (counter items only; bento sits in "daily food"), so the two chains' splits are NOT directly comparable — [FamilyMart Summary of selected financial data](https://www.family.co.jp/content/dam/family/english/ir/library/references/document/fm/summary_1011.pdf)

**Observed category tree in a typical SEJ store (listed by guides/travel sources; no sales figures attached)**
- Counter: Seven Café coffee/latte/smoothies, fried chicken ("Nanachiki"), karaage sticks, oden (winter), nikuman steamed buns, American dog — [Japan Travel: convenience store coffee guide](https://en.japantravel.com/article/japanese-convenience-store-coffee-ultimate-guide/71836)
- Chilled rice/noodles/sandwich: onigiri, bento, sushi rolls, pasta, cold noodles, sandwiches, egg sandwich, salads/cup deli — [SEJ Corporate Profile 2023-2024](https://www.sej.co.jp/library/common/pdf/en/e-yokogao2023-2024.pdf)
- Desserts/bakery: Seven Premium sweets, bread, pastries — same source
- Frozen: Seven Premium frozen meals, gyoza, ice cream — same source
- Beverages: ~50 soft-drink varieties on shelf — [Lal 2011, "Seven-Eleven Japan" (Stanford GSB case summary)](https://gwern.net/doc/japan/2011-lal.pdf)
- Alcohol, snacks/confectionery, instant noodles, daily goods, cosmetics, magazines, cigarettes (behind counter) — [Travel Pockets: inside a konbini](https://www.thetravelpockets.com/new-blog/what-is-inside-a-japanese-convenience-store-konbini)
- Services (not in merchandise sales): Seven Bank ATM, bill payment, ticket sales, parcel, 7NOW delivery — [SEJ Corporate Profile 2023-2024](https://www.sej.co.jp/library/common/pdf/en/e-yokogao2023-2024.pdf)

### Inferences
- Non-food is the largest single bucket by sales (32%) but the smallest by margin because cigarettes dominate it; cigarettes in Japan carry roughly 10–11% retail margin, which drags the whole bucket to 19.6%. Removing cigarettes, SEJ is a ~40%-gross-margin food business.
- The gap between processed-food margin (40.5%) and daily-food margin (34.8%) is unusual versus Western grocery and reflects Seven Premium's dominance in processed food.
- Fast food (SEJ definition) fell slightly in FY2024 (¥1,566 bn → ¥1,525 bn) while processed food rose; this coincides with the onigiri price hikes and the "Seven The Price" value push.

### Gaps
- SEJ does not publish a finer-than-four-bucket split (e.g., onigiri vs bento vs sandwiches). No reliable sub-category percentages found.
- The FY2024 Corporate Outline footnotes defining each bucket were not returned by the fetch; definitions above are from earlier editions of the same series.

## Q2. FamilyMart Japan: category split, margins, hero products, private label

### Takeaway
FamilyMart FY2024 (year to Feb 2025): fast food (counter) 6.2% of sales at 50.7% margin, daily food 27.9% at 37.4%, processed food 25.1% at 40.5%, non-food 30.7% at 15.4%, services 9.9%; blended merchandise margin 32.0%. Famichiki is the chain's single best-selling item (2.3 billion cumulative by Dec 2024).

- FY2025 (year to Feb 2026): record business profit ¥100.2 bn (+¥15.2 bn); existing-store daily sales up 54 consecutive months; growth attributed to Ohtani-fronted onigiri campaign, "Fuwa-uma" onigiri, white bread, chocolate sweets, Famichiki Red, cheese-in-Famichiki — [FamilyMart FY2025 results presentation, 8 Apr 2026](https://www.family.co.jp/content/dam/family/company/familymart/overview/260408_setumeishiryo.pdf)
- All-store average daily sales were ¥553,000 at 3Q FY2023 (Dec 2023) — [LIMO (Japanese retail news summary)](https://limo.media/articles/-/53970)

**Hero products**
- Famichiki: launched 17 Oct 2006 at ¥140, now ¥248; cumulative 1 bn by 2017, 2 bn by 2023 — [Unseen Japan](https://unseen-japan.com/famichiki-all-you-can-eat/); cumulative passed 2.3 bn in Dec 2024 — [FamilyMart Monthly Report Feb 2025](https://www.family.co.jp/content/dam/family/company/familymart/monthly_sales/getsuji_202502_japanese.pdf); 2.6 bn by May 2026 — [mkenzow Substack, "Why Famichiki still sells"](https://mkenzow.substack.com/p/why-familymarts-famichiki-still-sells)
- Famichiki Red (June 2025): 3 million in first week, ~30 million in six months — same Substack source
- Famichiki is FamilyMart's No. 1 product by sales across the entire chain — same source
- "Super chewy bread" series sells 4 units every second — [konbiniDB](https://konbinidb.com/en/news/familymart/370425/)
- Monthly report notes counter FF (Famichiki variants) and onigiri as the two categories driving sales in Feb 2025; PB-led frozen food, processed food and dry beverages also grew — [FamilyMart Monthly Report Feb 2025](https://www.family.co.jp/content/dam/family/company/familymart/monthly_sales/getsuji_202502_japanese.pdf)

**Private label and apparel**
- Famimaru: launched Oct 2021 consolidating multiple PBs into one name; 800+ items across food, beverages, daily necessities — [FamilyMart Sustainability: products that enrich lives](https://www.family.co.jp/english/sustainability/material_issues/needs/added_value.html)
- Convenience Wear (apparel): annual sales exceeded ¥13 bn in FY2024, >160% YoY; socks cumulative 24 million pairs (Feb 2025) — [AdverTimes, Aug 2025](https://www.advertimes.com/20250815/article508684/)
- Nikkei (Jan 2025): FamilyMart net profit +62% Mar–Nov 2024 with PB sweets strong — [Nikkei](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUC1563O0V10C25A1000000/)

### Inferences
- Famichiki at ~300 million/yr (2.3 bn Dec 2024 → 2.6 bn May 2026 ≈ 0.3 bn in ~17 months, roughly 210 m/yr by that arithmetic; cumulative pace of 1 bn per ~6 years also implies ~170–300 m/yr) works out to ~35–55 pieces per store per day across ~16,000 stores. Treat 300 m/yr as an upper-bound figure.
- Counter fast food is only 6% of FamilyMart sales but at 50.7% margin it delivers ~11% of gross profit — the highest-margin shelf in the store.
- FamilyMart's all-store daily sales are roughly 80% of SEJ's (≈¥570k vs ¥692k, FY2024; the FamilyMart number is an estimate extrapolated from ¥553k + ~3% growth).

### Gaps
- FamilyMart's FY2024/FY2025 absolute all-store daily sales (¥/day), customers/day and spend/customer were not in the fetched decks (image-based PDFs); only YoY percentages were readable.
- No published annual Famichiki unit count; only cumulative milestones. No Famima Café cup count found.
- Famimaru's share of sales or sales value was not found.

## Q3. 7-Eleven Thailand (CP All): product mix, margins, store economics

### Takeaway
CP All's 7-Eleven is a food-led format: 76% food & beverage / 24% non-food in 2024, ฿83,906/day per store from 978 customers spending ฿85 each; consolidated gross margin 22.6% (2024) → 22.7% (2025), dragged down by the Makro/Lotus's wholesale-retail segment.

### Cited Findings

**Product mix and margin**
- 2024 sales mix: food & beverage 76.0%, non-food 24.0%; F&B share rose YoY on new product launches and promotion of F&B — [CP All MD&A FY2024](https://www.cpall.co.th/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/MDA-EN-FY2024-V2.pdf) (via search summary; direct fetch blocked 403)
- Consolidated gross profit margin 22.6% in 2024 (21.9% in 2023); total revenue ฿987,794 m; net profit ฿25,346 m — same source
- 2025: revenue ฿1,022,143 m; GPM 22.7%; 15,945 Thai stores (+700); 112 Cambodia + 26 Laos; O2O (7Delivery + All Online) ~11% of sales — [SET Listed Company Snapshot, CPALL YE2025](https://lssmedia.setlink.set.or.th/2025/YE/CPALL-YE68-ListedCompanySnapshot-EN.html)
- Revenue composition 2025: convenience store 46%, wholesale (Makro) 48%, retail (Lotus's) 6% — same source
- Private brands ≈10% of CP All (7-Eleven) sales; margin strategy = expand ready-to-eat, drinks, personal care, household — [Thanachart Securities, Thailand Retail Sector, 11 Sep 2025, Ex. 21](https://tnsthinkwebcontent.thanachartsec.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/RETAIL250911E.pdf)
- 7-Eleven product differentiation: "strong food focus (76% foods : 24% non-foods), including ready-to-eat/ready-to-drink/ready-to-cook, with products extending to household and cooking products (All Grocer), personal care, IT gadgets, All Café beverage corners and eXta drugstores"; 80% of the 700 new 2024 stores were large standalone stores with parking "to accommodate the expanding number of SKUs" — same Thanachart report
- 7-Eleven holds ~70% of Thailand's 21,652 modern-trade CVS (15,245 of them, 2024), up from 61% in 2014 — same Thanachart report (citing CPALL 2024 Annual Report)

**Store economics**

- Alcohol: Chang, Singha beer; wine; spirits behind counter; RTDs ฿40–57; legal sale windows 11–14h and 17–24h
- Ice cream: Wall's Cornetto range

### Inferences
- ฿83,906/day ÷ 978 customers = ฿85.8 ticket, consistent with the published ฿85. At ~฿37/USD that is ≈US$2,270/day/store, about one-sixth of SEJ's ¥692k (≈US$4,600) and 40% of 7-Eleven US merchandise-only ($5,657).
- CVS-segment gross margin is materially higher than the 22.6% consolidated figure (which blends in Makro wholesale at ~11% GM); CP All does not disclose the CVS-only GM separately in the sources found. Analysts' "76% food" framing plus 10% PB suggests a CVS GM in the high-20s %, but this is an estimate.
- The ฿85 ticket with ~1,000 customers/day is the Thai pattern closest to an Indian kirana/convenience context: high footfall, tiny basket, food-and-drink-led.

### Gaps
- CP All's 56-1 One Report and MD&A were 403-blocked; the 76/24 split and per-store figures come from search engine extracts of the MD&A, not a direct read.
- No published unit volumes for toasties, All Café cups, or ready-to-eat meals.
- No published average store size (sqm) or SKU count for 7-Eleven Thailand; "~100 sqm" appears only as a generic CVS reference in secondary sources.

## Q4. Taiwan: 7-ELEVEN (President Chain Store) and FamilyMart Taiwan

### Takeaway
Taiwan's two chains are the most coffee- and fresh-food-intensive 7-Eleven/FamilyMart markets: PCSC's CITY CAFE alone grosses NT$17 bn (2023) and its fresh-food target was NT$50 bn for 2024; Taiwan FamilyMart's fresh food is >30% of revenue (2022) and Let's Café sells ~170 million cups a year.

### Cited Findings

**7-ELEVEN Taiwan (PCSC, ticker 2912)**

- Taiwan 7-ELEVEN = 62% of group revenue and 47% of pre-tax profit (9M 2024); 7,066 stores (Nov 2024); fresh-food revenue target NT$50 bn for 2024; OPENPOINT members >18 m contributing >50% of sales with 1.6× the ticket of non-members; net new-store target 200–250/yr — [Fugle, PCSC earnings call notes, Dec 2024](https://blog.fugle.tw/post/earnings-call-2912-2024-12-23)

- Tea eggs: ~40 million sold per year in 7-Eleven alone; price raised to NT$10 (2016) — [Focus Taiwan, Jan 2016](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/201601210028)
- 2024: online services = 11.59% of transactions and 6.2% of standalone revenue; fresh-food sub-brands Star Rated Cuisine, Veggie Selection (16.4 m units in 2024), Ohlala; "Simple-Fit" <500 kcal fresh meals; fresh-food lines include rice, fresh noodles, salads, cooked food, refrigerated bread, sweet potatoes; FUN vending machines sell 4°C/18°C items, CITY CAFE, hot boxed meals — [PCSC 2024 Annual Report (ESG/business section)](https://ir.7-11.com.tw/taiwan/2912/annual2/2024/EN/20250805114533-1.pdf)
- Per-store: 6,859 outlets averaging TWD 30.1 m (US$940k) sales per store per year in 2024 → ≈NT$82k/day (calc.) — [USDA FAS Taiwan Retail Foods Annual 2025](https://apps.fas.usda.gov/newgainapi/api/Report/DownloadReportByFileName?fileName=Retail+Foods+Annual_Taipei+ATO_Taiwan_TW2025-0024.pdf) (via search summary; not directly verified)
- Typical Taiwan 7-ELEVEN counter/fresh tree (guides): tea eggs, oden/關東煮, roasted sweet potatoes, steamed buns, CITY CAFE, bento, onigiri, sandwiches, fresh fruit cups, hot dogs, plus iCash/ibon kiosks for tickets/bills/parcels — [Taiwanderers](https://taiwanderers.com/taiwan-7-eleven-food-drink-souvenirs/); [Taipei Tourism](https://www.taipeitourism.org/taiwan-convenience-stores/)

**FamilyMart Taiwan (Taiwan FamilyMart Co., ticker 5903)**
- 4,288 stores (Sep 2024) — [Taiwan FamilyMart company profile Q3 2024](https://www.family.com.tw/Web_EnterPrise/Content/PDF/113company%20profile-Q3英文簡報.pdf) (via search summary; direct fetch 403)
- Fresh-food revenue NT$25 bn in 2022, >30% of total revenue; fresh food growing ≥10%/yr for three years — same source

### Inferences
- PCSC's fresh-food target of NT$50 bn against CVS revenue of ~NT$211 bn implies fresh food ≈ 24% of 7-ELEVEN Taiwan sales (2024), and CITY CAFE ≈ 8%. Combined counter+fresh ≈ one-third of sales — similar to Japan's fast-food bucket.
- At NT$17 bn and ~300 m cups, CITY CAFE averages ~NT$55–60/cup and ~115 cups/store/day — the same order as Seven Café Japan's ~120 cups/store/day.
- 40 m tea eggs/yr ÷ ~5,000 stores (2016) ≈ 22 eggs/store/day at NT$10 — tiny revenue, large traffic/habit role.

### Gaps
- Neither PCSC nor Taiwan FamilyMart publishes a Japan-style category-by-category sales/margin table in English; the PCSC PDF retrieved was the ESG-heavy annual report, not the financial supplement.
- No verified figure for PCSC per-store daily sales (PSD), customers/day, store size (ping), or SKU count.
- "Beverages ~60% of store revenue, food 36%" appears in one secondary snippet without a verifiable source; excluded as unreliable.

## Q5. 7-Eleven USA: contrast with Asia

### Takeaway
7-Eleven Inc's store sales are 61% fuel by value; within merchandise, processed food (packaged beverages, snacks, candy) is 47% and non-food (cigarettes, lottery, etc.) 36%, with fast food only 13% and daily food 4% — the inverse of Japan. Merchandise gross margin is 33.3% (2024).

### Cited Findings
- FY2024 (Dec 2024) 7-Eleven Inc sales by category (¥ m): processed food 1,923,604; fast food 551,086; daily food 162,592; non-food 1,476,574; merchandise total 4,113,857; fuel 6,379,433 — [Seven & i Corporate Outline 2025 – Overseas CVS](https://www.7andi.com/en/ir/library/co_financial/2025/convenience_store_overseas.html)
- Calculated shares (inference): fuel 60.8% of total; of merchandise — processed food 46.8%, fast food 13.4%, daily food 4.0%, non-food 35.9%

## Q7. SKU counts, assortment depth and churn

### Takeaway
A Japanese 7-Eleven carries ~2,800–3,000 SKUs in ~140 sqm, replaces ~70% of them within a year, and receives ~80–100 new items a week; per sub-category, depth is deliberately shallow (e.g., ~50 soft-drink facings chosen from 200+ brands).

### Cited Findings
- Average store carries about 3,000 products — [Wikipedia: Seven-Eleven Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven-Eleven_Japan); "over 3,000 SKUs that change by time of day, day of week, and season" — [Porter Prize 2003 citation](https://www.porterprize.org/english/pastwinner/2003/12/03145411.html)
- Headquarters recommends ~80 new items a week, ~4,000 new items a year — [UT Dallas SEJ case facts](https://personal.utdallas.edu/~metin/Or6366/Folios/IntroStrategy/scseveneleven.pdf)
- ~200 new products hit konbini shelves every week (industry-wide); ~70% of products replaced within a year — [TJF Takarabako: konbini](https://tjf.or.jp/takarabako/japaneseculture/28konbini.htm); 70% annual replacement also in [Lal 2011](https://gwern.net/doc/japan/2011-lal.pdf)
- Soft drinks: >200 brands available in market; a 7-Eleven displays ~50 varieties with ~70% annual turnover — [Lal 2011, Seven-Eleven Japan](https://gwern.net/doc/japan/2011-lal.pdf)
- Seven Premium alone ≈ 3,460 items group-wide (Feb 2025), so a single store carries only a subset — [Seven & i press release, Apr 2025](https://www.7andi.com/company/news/release/202504161100___.html)
- Thailand: 80% of 2024's 700 new stores were larger standalone formats "to accommodate the expanding number of SKUs" — [Thanachart](https://tnsthinkwebcontent.thanachartsec.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/RETAIL250911E.pdf)
- Seven & i is exploring larger-format 7-Elevens in Japan (SIP stores) with wider fresh/grocery range — [Japan Times, Feb 2024](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2024/02/29/companies/japan-7-eleven-bigger-store/)

### Inferences
- 3,000 SKUs in 143 sqm ≈ 21 SKUs/sqm; a 60–75 sqm (600–800 sq ft) Indian store at the same density would carry ~1,300–1,600 SKUs.
- "How many brands per item": the soft-drink example (50 facings from 200+ brands) is the only hard data point found; it implies 2–4 brands per flavour/sub-type, not the 8–10 common in Indian supermarkets.

### Gaps
- No current (2023–26) published SKU count per store from SEJ, FamilyMart, CP All or PCSC; the 3,000 figure is repeated across secondary sources and academic cases.
- No data on the number of brands per sub-category (colas, instant noodles) beyond the dated soft-drink example; no fresh/daily-vs-ambient SKU split found.
