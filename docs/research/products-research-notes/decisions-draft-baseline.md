# Consto — Decision Checklist (baseline draft)

**Draft v0.1 · 6 October 2026 · for Sateesh · built from Products.md and research notes 01–09**

## How to use this

1. Every line is one decision. Read the question, read the one-line "why", look at my pick.
2. My pick is a firm assumption, not a test. If it is right, write "OK" after "Your answer". If not, write what you want instead.
3. Nothing here says "try it for 60 days". Where I had to guess a number, the guess is in brackets as (assumption: …). Change the number and the pick changes with it.
4. Start with the 12 Big Calls. Every other question leans on them. If a Big Call changes, tell me and I rewrite the sections that depend on it.
5. Then go theme by theme, A to U. You can do one theme a day; each stands on its own.
6. Numbers in square brackets come from the research, with the year, e.g. [Japan: waste 2–3% of sales, 2020]. "est." means I derived it from published numbers.
7. "Dept" numbers point to consto-category-master.md. Letter codes (F2, R2, PR1, RS5) point to consto-rulebook.md. I stayed inside your existing decisions unless I say so in the line.
8. Rupee prices are Hyderabad 2025–26 unless marked. Replace any with your own quotes.
9. "Vendor" means an outside maker who cooks and delivers. The store never cooks (rule F1).
10. When you are done, send the file back. I turn your answers into the opening plan, the vendor specs and the POS catalogue.
