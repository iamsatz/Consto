**CEO's file · 6 October 2026 · built from `products-research-notes/decisions-draft-baseline.md`, the six persona notes, `Products.md` §7, §11, §12 and `consto-rulebook.md` ch. 14**

## How to use this file

1. Answer the Big Calls in section 1 first. Every line in section 3 leans on them; if a Big Call changes, tell me and I rewrite the lines that depend on it.
2. Every pick in this file is an assumption, mine, made after reading the six personas argue. Nothing says "test it". Where I guessed a number it is in brackets as (assumption: …); change the number and the pick changes with it.
3. Section 2 is different: those are places where my ruling would change a rule you already wrote in the rulebook or the category master. I recommend; you decide.
4. Write your answer in the blank after "Your answer:". "OK" means you accept my pick. Anything else is your pick, and it overrules mine.
5. Where the personas disagreed with each other, the line says so in a short bracket, e.g. (Designer wanted X; Investor Y), so you can see the debate without opening six files.
6. The research behind each number is in `Products.md` and the notes folder (`01`–`09`); the persona arguments are in `persona-*.md`; the neutral baseline you compared against is `decisions-draft-baseline.md`.
7. "Vendor" means an outside kitchen that cooks and delivers. "Mana Ooru" is the trust shelf. "Saayantram pack" is chai plus two hot pieces for ₹30. Letter codes (F1, P2, R3, RS4) point to the rulebook; "Dept" numbers to the category master.
8. Rupee figures are Hyderabad 2025–26 and every price marked ~ is a quote you must replace after a phone call. Section 5 lists the eight facts no answer in this file can settle.
9. Do one theme a day in section 3 (A to U); each stands on its own. Section 4 is one page of numbers so you can see what your answers cost.
10. When you are done, send the file back. Your answers become the opening plan, the vendor specs, the roster, the POS catalogue and the Agent's rules.
