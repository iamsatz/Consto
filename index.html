Hello. I am Sateesh, and I am building Consto.

India's first AI-backed neighbourhood store. The warmth of a kirana, the discipline of a 7-Eleven, and an AI memory that genuinely remembers each customer over years.

01Where this idea came from

I spent 15 years behind my father's counter.

From the age of seven to twenty-two, I stood inside the family kirana in Gadwal, Telangana. I knew every regular customer's children by name. I watched my father extend credit to families who could not pay on time, never with embarrassment. I saw trust compound into loyalty that lasted decades.

In 2007, I stepped away from the kirana. From that moment, my observation began. For 18+ years I watched Indian retail change. I watched DMart strip the local soul out of retail to scale. I watched Zepto try to win without ever knowing the customer. I watched 200,000 kiranas close in two years.

Earlier this year, in February and March 2026, I travelled to Thailand and Taiwan. I walked into 7-Eleven and FamilyMart stores. Operationally brilliant. Multi-service, fast, well-stocked. But nobody knew my name. Emotionally empty.

That trip is when every dot connected. The kirana had warmth but no scale. Convenience stores had scale but no warmth. Quick commerce had speed but no soul. Nobody in India had built the combination.

I spent the months since interviewing 50+ families, drivers, retirees, and professionals in Beeramguda. They all said the same thing in different words. Nobody remembers what I need.

That is the problem Consto solves.

02What I have seen

Four people. One unmet need.

Across very different lives, the same emotional gap kept showing up. These four people became the personas every product decision is tested against.

A working mother, 35

"I spend forty minutes every evening figuring out what to cook, what to buy, whether the milk is fresh, whether I forgot the electricity bill."

A gig driver, 28

"The bank queue takes thirty minutes for ₹2000 cash. I lose two rides. Every time."

A retiree, 55+

"My grandson installed four apps for me. I cannot use any of them. The kirana boy is rude. Nobody is patient with me."

A tech professional, 30

"BigBasket is fine, but I never know what is actually fresh. And I cannot ask them anything. They do not know me."

03What Consto is

A physical store. Three pillars. One AI memory.

A 1,000 to 1,500 sqft physical neighbourhood store in Beeramguda. Three pillars hold it up. Three format variants adapt it to location. One AI memory layer powers everything.

Pillar 01

The Memory Layer

Every customer gets a dedicated AI agent. It learns from purchases, timing, family signals, festival rhythms. The agent never speaks to the customer. It equips human staff with curated insights. Humans deliver warmth. AI scales memory.

Pillar 02

One-Stop Services

Groceries, AEPS cash withdrawals, bill payments, mobile recharges, OTC medicines, fresh batter, festival items. Five daily-life tasks in one 15-minute visit. Roughly 40% of revenue from services.

Pillar 03

Community Anchoring

Festival calendar. Monthly Hub Huddle events. "Come rest even if you do not buy" policy. Credit with dignity. Policies that are inherently worth talking about. Marketing budget becomes word-of-mouth.
