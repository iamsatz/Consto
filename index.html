About this work.

A note before you start reading.
Consto is a 1,000-1,500 sqft physical neighbourhood store. Three format variants — Daily, Home, Hub — adapt to where each store sits. An AI memory layer at the centre that genuinely remembers each customer's family, festivals, and rhythm over years of compounding interaction.

The work is grounded in 15 years inside a family kirana, ~18 years of structured retail observation across India and Asia, and 50+ customer interviews in Beeramguda, Hyderabad. Not theory.
